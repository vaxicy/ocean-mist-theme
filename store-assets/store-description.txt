Ocean Mist Theme is a calm, minimalist Chrome theme inspired by quiet coastlines and Nordic design. It wraps your browser in a soft palette of deep ocean blue, sky blue, mist cyan, and warm white — creating a premium, comfortable workspace without gradients, patterns, or illustrations.

The deep ocean blue frame feels stable and professional, while the warm white toolbar and active tab keep the interface bright and easy to read. Soft sky blue accents guide your eyes through inactive tabs, and the mist cyan bookmark bar adds a gentle layer of calm.

Designed for long browsing sessions, Ocean Mist avoids high-saturation colors and visual noise. Whether you are working, reading, or researching, it keeps your Chrome window quiet, clean, and focused.

Install Ocean Mist Theme to bring the stillness of a morning shore to every new tab.
