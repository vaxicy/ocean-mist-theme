=== Short Description (Software Package Summary) ===
A calm, minimalist Chrome theme inspired by the morning coastline — deep ocean blue, soft sky blue, mist cyan, and warm white for focus.

=== English Detailed Description ===
Ocean Mist Theme brings the quiet feeling of a morning coastline into your browser. Inspired by soft sea fog, gentle sky light, and deep ocean horizons, this minimalist Chrome theme uses a palette of deep ocean blue, soft sky blue, mist cyan, and warm white. The result is a clean, comfortable workspace that helps you stay calm and focused.

There are no gradients, no textures, and no illustrations — just solid colors that reduce visual noise and keep your tabs, bookmarks, and new tab page looking tidy. The warm white toolbar creates a soft contrast against the deep blue frame, while subtle cyan accents add coastal freshness.

Ocean Mist is perfect for anyone who loves minimalist design, coastal aesthetics, or simply wants a quieter browser environment. Whether you are working, studying, or exploring the web, this theme wraps your Chrome window in a gentle atmosphere. Install Ocean Mist Theme and bring a sense of calm to every new tab.
